Account Safety

What happens if something goes wrong

  • If your session expires, RMIMS will return you to the login page instead of showing incomplete account data.
  • If your account record cannot be loaded, your information will not be replaced with fake or blank values.
  • Logging out is protected against repeated clicks while the request is processing.
  • Your User settings cannot access Admin-only backup, restore, reset, or system-management actions.
