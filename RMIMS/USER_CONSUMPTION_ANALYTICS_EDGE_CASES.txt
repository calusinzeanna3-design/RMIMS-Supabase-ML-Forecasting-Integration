RMIMS USER CONSUMPTION ANALYTICS — EDGE CASE UPDATE

Applied to user/analytics.html, js/user-analytics.js, and css/user-analytics.css.

Handled cases:
- No consumption records
- No records in selected date range
- Only one material consumed
- Mixed units are never added together
- Inconsistent units for the same material are flagged
- Zero/negative/invalid usage records are excluded from analytics
- Duplicate submissions are not artificially combined by analytics
- Archived materials keep historical consumption visibility
- Missing/orphan material references are excluded and reported
- Invalid date ranges are blocked
- Future historical periods are blocked
- Tied highest/lowest consumption is represented without arbitrary selection
- Large and decimal quantities are formatted safely
- Database/load failure is distinguished from zero consumption
- Forecast readiness is shown only as a data-readiness notice
- Trend is not calculated when there is insufficient history
- Consumption trend is not combined across kg, pcs, L, etc.
- Current stock status remains separate from consumption quantity

IMPORTANT:
No new SQL migration is required for these UI/analytics edge-case protections.
The page uses the existing materials and usage_records tables.
