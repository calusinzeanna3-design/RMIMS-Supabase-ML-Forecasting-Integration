ABOUT RM(S)ME

What is RM(S)ME?

RM(S)ME is a web-based raw materials inventory management system designed to help micro, small, and medium enterprises record inventory data, monitor material movement, understand consumption patterns, and use AI-based forecasting for better inventory planning.
