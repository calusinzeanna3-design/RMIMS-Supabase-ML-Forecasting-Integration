Why we built this

Made for the way small businesses actually work.

Most MSMEs still track raw materials on paper logs or scattered spreadsheets — until a shortage shows up with no warning, or nobody can say what came in and out last week.

RMSME exists to close that gap without the overhead of an enterprise system: a front desk that logs every material as it moves, and a back office that always knows what's on the shelf and what's running low.

How it works

One system, two everyday jobs.

Admins set the system up and keep watch. Staff log what moves through the door. Here's the generic path each one follows.

Step 1 of 4

Set up your inventory

Add your raw materials, organize them into categories, and set the reorder thresholds that matter to your business.

Step 2 of 4

Add your team

Create staff accounts and assign roles, so each person sees exactly the tools relevant to their job.

Step 3 of 4

Monitor in real time

Watch stock levels and material activity from the dashboard as material moves.

Step 4 of 4

Review & report

Pull usage records and reports whenever you need them, for restocking decisions or audits.

Step 1 of 4

Check in

Tap in at the front desk when you arrive with material to drop off or pick up.

Step 2 of 4

Log the material

Record the drop-off or pick-up in a few taps — pick the material, enter the quantity, done.

Step 3 of 4

Confirm the details

Double-check the quantity and material before submitting, so the record is right the first time.

Step 4 of 4

You're routed

Get sent to the right desk or task automatically — no waiting on someone to look it up.
